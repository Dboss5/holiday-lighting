A FEW BRIGHT QUESTIONS

Before the first twinkle.

Can I mix roofline, tree, and pathway lighting?

Yes—use the planner to collect the features you like, then discuss how they can work together at your property. The final design depends on access, power, and installation requirements.

Will the preview show my actual house?

This is an interactive illustration to explore color and placement. It is not a photo of an installation or a custom rendering. Bring photos of your property when discussing your design.

What will my installation cost?

Request a property-specific quote. Building size, roofline access, display choices, and installation needs affect the plan. This planner does not calculate or promise a price.

Can the lights work for more than Christmas?

Explore Halloween designs and color-changing smart LEDs in our lighting collection. Ask which options fit your property and the occasions you have in mind.

How do I share my saved plan?

Save your lighting brief, then call 945-777-3345 to discuss it. Saving keeps a copy on your device; it does not send a request or reserve an installation.
